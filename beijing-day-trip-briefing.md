## Suggested One-Day Itinerary (quick guide)

1. **8:00 AM** — Start at Tiananmen Square Area, then walk through the Meridian Gate (entrance to the Forbidden City).
2. **8:30 AM opening** — Enter the Palace Museum; work north through the Hall of Supreme Harmony, the Three Great Halls, the Imperial Garden.
3. **Lunch** — Grab something quick inside or near the compound; Wangfujing is a short metro ride away.
4. **Afternoon** — Exit the North gate toward Jingshan Park for a panoramic view over the Forbidden City rooftops (a classic photo spot).
5. **~4:30 PM** — Last entry cut-off; be out by closing time.
6. Optional wrap: **Night** — Wangfujing Street or a Beijing roast duck dinner.
